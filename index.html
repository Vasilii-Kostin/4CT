🇬🇧 Welcome

This project is dedicated to a constructive proof of the Four Color Theorem and an explicit algorithm for coloring planar graphs.

What is here?
  • Theory — a brief outline of the proof based on forced chains.
  • Demo — a working algorithm that colors graphs with 4 colors.
  • Paper — the full proof (PDF, Zenodo).

The project shows that the Four Color Theorem is not just about existence, but a constructive process that can be explained and performed manually.

📄 Full paper on Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21514884
